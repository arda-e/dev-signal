# DevSignal Terms of Use

Review draft, 6 November 2026. Operator/contact: [u/ardico](https://www.reddit.com/user/ardico/). Check against the actual deployment and publish this document before review.

DevSignal provides automated technical discussion briefs for a personal, non-commercial learning project and public Reddit readers. A brief represents a limited sample, may omit context or contain errors, and is not proof of a growing trend. Check the linked sources and verify claims independently.

Source content belongs to its respective authors. Summary posts identify the original discussion and disclose automation. The app is not affiliated with or endorsed by Reddit, OpenAI or Supabase. Availability and allowed features depend on those services and applicable review decisions.

The app does not train models, resell data, vote, message or publish to LinkedIn automatically. It does not write in source communities. Public digests and optional private storage are described in the Privacy Policy. Send correction/removal requests and app issues to the operator with the relevant link.
