# Ardico Reading Inbox Terms of Use

Last updated: 7 November 2026. Operator/contact: [u/ardico](https://www.reddit.com/user/ardico/).

Ardico Reading Inbox is a personal, non-commercial assistant for finding and reading public technical discussions in r/ClaudeAI and r/ChatGPT. Devvit collects selected posts and bounded comment samples inside Reddit and can deliver them to a private Supabase inbox when external transfer is enabled and permitted. External transfer is currently disabled pending the Reddit domain exception review. A sample may omit replies, older comments or truncated text; it is not a complete discussion or proof of an emerging trend. Check the linked sources.

Source content belongs to its respective authors. The app is not affiliated with or endorsed by Reddit or Supabase. Availability, external transfers and the intended later personal AI analysis depend on applicable terms and review decisions. This document does not claim approval.

The collector makes no AI calls, trains no models, resells no data, votes or messages, writes no source-community content and publishes no generated summaries or LinkedIn posts. The existing Web template's separate custom-post creation behaviour remains. The later AI processor and Notion integration are not implemented. Data handling and operator removal responsibilities are described in the [Privacy Policy](https://github.com/arda-e/dev-signal/blob/main/docs/privacy.md).

Send correction/removal requests and app issues to the operator with the relevant source link.
